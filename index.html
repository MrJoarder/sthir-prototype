How Sthir works

Three steps between a family and the care they need.

01

Share your location

One tap gives you live distance and ETA to every partner hospital, ambulance, and blood bank nearby — no address typing.

02

Compare in real time

See per-day ICU rates, free beds, ambulance type, and blood units side by side — sorted by what's actually closest and available.

03

Book or dispatch instantly

Reserve an ICU bed, call an ambulance, or request blood — get a confirmation ID immediately, with no back-and-forth calls.
