Questions

Frequently asked

Is the availability shown actually real-time?

In this prototype, bed counts and blood units are mock data refreshed on load. In production, each partner facility updates their own dashboard, and figures sync to Sthir within a couple of minutes.

What happens after I book an ICU bed?

You get a booking ID immediately and the hospital's admissions desk calls the contact number you provided to confirm and guide check-in.

Can I request blood without a Sthir account?

Yes — blood and ambulance requests only need a working phone number. Accounts are only needed to view your booking history later.

How are hospitals verified?

Partner facilities go through a registration check against government hospital licenses before they can list beds or rates.
